House rules
Printed here so nobody has to look for them.
4.1

Chronological, always.

No feed, no ranking, no "recommended for you". The newest thing is on top. Everybody gets the same board.

4.2

Local only.

Listings are tied to one of Chicago's 77 community areas. If you can't walk, bike, or take the Blue Line to it, it's not here.

4.3

Direct dealing.

You talk to your neighbor. Meet on the stoop, in daylight, with cash or a handshake. Stoop takes nothing.
