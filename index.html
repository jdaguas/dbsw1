Stoop: 25 tries at a better craigslist.

The same idea, redesigned twenty-five times: a local classifieds board with no fees and no algorithm, where you deal directly with your neighbors. The first round goes as wide as possible. The later rounds narrow in on what actually serves the people using it.

Who it's forCity residents buying, selling, giving away, or looking for a room or a gig nearby.
Should understandThis is a plain, trustworthy, local board. No middleman.
Should doBrowse a category or search, or post a listing in under a minute.
Plain HTML & CSS, no frameworks. Click any thumbnail to open that version. Every page links back here.
