To Place an Ad — Free

Takes less than one minute. No account, no fee, no salesman will call.

  1. Choose a heading — For Sale, Free, Housing, Help Wanted, Gigs, Services, Community, or Lost & Found.
  2. Write it plain. What it is, what it costs, which neighborhood. Abbreviate if you like; we all do.
  3. Leave a way to reach you. Replies come straight to you, never through us.
  4. That’s it. It runs in tomorrow’s board, in the order received. Renews free every 14 days.
